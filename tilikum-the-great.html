Tilikum The Great

Tilikum the Great is a loop based improvisational noise rock duo based in Leeds. The music they create darts from extreme to extreme flowing from chaotic noisy improvisations to tightly structured noise rock songs. Tilikum the great creates an intense atmosphere at every show they play pushing the limited rig they have to the breaking point and delivering a vastly different show every night.
