Alex Izaak Milling

Cardigan Road

Released: 14/06/25

Recorded, performed, mixed and mastered at Cardigan Road, Leeds by Alex Izaak Milling via Blameus.Notthem* Studios

This is a short record dedicated to the place I've been living the last three years, and the people I've shared said space with. Over this time I feel like I've done a lot of growing as an artist & person, all the while being facilitated by Cardigan Road. It had character and quirks, low ceilings that many fell victim to, and walls that have heard many a conversation that has shifted my perception on improvisation (a long with who I am as an individual)l. I have had some of the best and worst moments in this space, and it seems fitting that the last thing I did there before moving out was record this audio. It's not perfect or polished, but neither was my time in Cardigan Road.
