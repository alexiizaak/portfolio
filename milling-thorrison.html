Alex Izaak Milling

Milling // Thorrison

Alex Izaak Milling and Hinrik Þórisson form a restless guitar-and-drums duo devoted to free improvisation and exploratory sound. Their performances unfold as raw, kinetic energy, where jagged rhythms and fractured harmonies give way to sudden moments of silence and clarity. Drawing on extended techniques, dense pedal work, and the volatile energy of noise, they construct uneven and shifting sets that refuse easy categorization, falling somewhere in between jazz, rock and noise music. In the lineage of the rich extended improvised scene emerging from the North East of England, Milling and Þórisson forge a voice that is unique unmistakably them.
