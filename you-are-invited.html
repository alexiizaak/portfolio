Tilikum the Great is a loop based improvisational noise rock duo based in Leeds. The music they create darts between extremes flowing from chaotic noisy improvisations to tightly structured noise rock songs. On the 16th of June 2022 they attempted to capture the same energy in a recorded room, and then decorate around it whilst still keeping true to their sound. This is the result.
